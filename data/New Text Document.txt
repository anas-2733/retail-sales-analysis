# Retail Sales Analysis: Project Notes

1. Objective
Analyse retail sales performance to identify profitable categories, regional
strengths and weaknesses, loss-making products, and seasonal sales trends.

2. Dataset
- Source: Sample Superstore (public dataset)
- Size: 9,994 records, 21 columns
- Period: January 2014 to December 2017
- Tools: Excel, MySQL, Python (Pandas), Power BI

3. Data Quality Checks
- Missing values: none
- Duplicate records: none (one record repeats if Row ID is ignored)
- Order Date was stored as text (MM/DD/YYYY); converted to a proper date
  type using the English (United States) locale in Power Query.

4. Key Metrics
| Metric        | Value        |
|---------------|--------------|
| Total Sales   | 2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit  | 286,397.02   |
| Total Orders  | 5,009        |
| Profit Margin | 12.47%       |

5. Key Findings
1. Category: Furniture generates 741,999.80 in sales but only 18,451.27 in
   profit. Technology leads in both sales (836,154.03) and profit (145,454.95).
2. Loss-making sub-categories: Tables (-17,725), Bookcases (-3,473) and
   Supplies (-1,189). Tables and Bookcases explain Furniture's low profit.
3. Region: West performs best (sales 725,458; profit 108,418). Central has
   the weakest margin at about 7.9%, despite sales of 501,240.
4. Seasonality: Sales peak in September, November and December every year.
   February is the weakest month.
5. Growth: Sales dipped about 3% in 2015, then grew about 29% in 2016 and
   about 20% in 2017.

6. Recommendations (preliminary)
- Review pricing and discounting on Tables and Bookcases.
- Investigate why Central's margin is much lower than other regions.
- Plan inventory and promotions around the September to December peak.

7. Pending Analysis
- Relationship between discount and profit (Python)
- Loss-making orders and customers (SQL)
- Interactive dashboard (Power BI)




## SQL Analysis (MySQL)

### Setup
- Database: retail_sales, table: superstore (9,994 rows, 21 columns)
- Data was loaded with a generated SQL script. The Workbench Import Wizard
  skipped about 300 rows because of the file's latin1 encoding, so it was
  not used.
- Dates were stored as DATE type (source format MM/DD/YYYY).
- Postal codes were stored as text and padded to 5 digits, because the CSV
  had dropped leading zeros (449 records).
- Validation: row count 9,994, total sales 2,297,200.86 and total profit
  286,397.02 matched the Excel results.
- Script: sql/analysis_queries.sql

### Queries and Results
| # | Question                          | Technique                       | Result |
|---|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------|--------|
| 1 | Overall KPIs                      | SUM, COUNT(DISTINCT)            | Sales 2,297,200.86; Profit 286,397.02; Orders 5,009 |
| 2 | Sales and profit by category      | GROUP BY, ORDER BY              | Technology 836,154 / 145,455; Furniture 741,999 / 18,451; Office Supplies 719,047 / 122,491 |
| 3 | Performance by region             | GROUP BY, ORDER BY              | West 108,418 profit; East 91,523; South 46,749; Central 39,706 |
| 4 | Loss-making sub-categories        | GROUP BY, HAVING                | Tables -17,725; Bookcases -3,473; Supplies -1,189 |
| 5 | Monthly sales trend               | YEAR(), MONTH(), GROUP BY       | 48 months (2014 to 2017) |
| 6 | Discount band vs profit           | CASE WHEN, GROUP BY             | See finding 1 |
| 7 | Top 10 loss-making products       | GROUP BY, ORDER BY, LIMIT       | See finding 2 |
| 8 | Top 10 customers by sales         | COUNT(DISTINCT), GROUP BY       | See finding 3 |
| 9 | Order lines that lose money       | WHERE                           | 1,871 lines; total loss 156,131.29 |

### Key Findings
1. Discount is the strongest driver of loss.
   | Discount band | Order lines | Margin  |
   |---------------|-------------|---------|
   | 0%            | 4,798       | 29.51%  |
   | 1-20%         | 3,803       | 11.91%  |
   | 21-40%        | 460         | -15.30% |
   | Above 40%     | 933         | -77.40% |
   The 1,393 lines with discounts above 20% lose about 135,376 combined.
2. The largest product losses are the Cubify CubeX 3D Printer, Double Head
   (-8,880), the Lexmark MX611dhe Laser Printer (-4,590) and the Cubify CubeX
   3D Printer, Triple Head (-3,840). Heavy losses are not limited to
   Furniture.
3. The top customer by sales, Sean Miller (25,043), is unprofitable at
   -1,981. Tamara Chand (19,052) and Raymond Buch (15,117) are profitable.
4. 1,871 order lines (about 19% of all lines) lose money, with a total loss
   of 156,131, which is more than half of the total profit of 286,397.

### Recommendations (SQL stage)
- Cap or review discounts above 20%, as they consistently produce losses.
- Review pricing on the Cubify 3D printers, the Lexmark printer, Tables and
  Bookcases.
- Check the discount pattern in Sean Miller's orders before offering
  further discounts to high-volume customers.
